About

The artist behind the box.

My name is Amanda King! I'm a self-taught artist based in North Carolina who's been working with all kinds of mediums for over 20 years. Art has always been my way of expressing myself. Whether I'm drawing, making music, or acting behind the camera, I just love creating, all across the board!

I've shaped my gift over the years through trial and error, practicing for hours at a time until my technique finally clicked (though I still have so much to learn, and I love that).

I hope having a little bit of everything here keeps things exciting and unique for you. There's still so much more in store as I keep building up my collection every day. I hope you love what you see! Thank you so much for stopping by!
