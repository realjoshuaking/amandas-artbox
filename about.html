About

The artist behind the box.

Hi, I'm Amanda. I draw and paint animals, nature, and little bits of whimsy, working mostly in ink, charcoal, and acrylic.

Most of the pieces here started as sketchbook drawings. I love detailed pen work and soft charcoal shading, and I paint in full color when a piece calls for it.

Everything in the gallery is an original, made by hand. When a piece finds its home, it's gone for good.

If a piece speaks to you, don't overthink it.
