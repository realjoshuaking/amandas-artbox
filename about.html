About

The artist behind the box.

Hi, I'm Amanda. I paint in the color I've loved my whole life: purple.

My work lives somewhere between calm and joyful. I paint florals, skies, and quiet little worlds, mostly in acrylics, sometimes in watercolor or mixed media when the mood strikes.

Everything in the gallery is an original, made by hand in my studio. No prints, no reproductions. When a piece finds its home, it's gone for good, which makes every sale a little bittersweet and completely wonderful.

If a piece speaks to you, don't overthink it. Art is at its best when it just feels right.
